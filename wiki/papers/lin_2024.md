## Summary
This is a step-by-step computational protocol for processing zebrafish single-nucleus ATAC-seq data that demonstrates use of ArchR workflows with TF-IDF + SVD (LSI) for dimensionality reduction, sample integration, peak calling, and downstream motif enrichment and footprinting. It supplies procedures to construct a zebrafish-specific TF motif PWM database and integrate it into peak-level motif enrichment and TF footprinting analyses to infer regulatory activity during early embryogenesis.

## Hypothesis framed
A reproducible ArchR-based pipeline using TF‑IDF + SVD (LSI) dimensionality reduction combined with a curated zebrafish-specific motif database enables robust identification of cluster-specific accessible regions, TF motif enrichments, and footprint evidence of TF binding in zebrafish early embryonic snATAC-seq data.

## Questions answered
- Can an ArchR-based TF‑IDF + SVD (LSI) pipeline be applied to zebrafish snATAC-seq to recover cluster-specific accessible regions and enable downstream motif enrichment/footprinting?
- How can a researcher construct and integrate a zebrafish-specific TF motif PWM database for motif enrichment and footprinting analyses on snATAC-seq peaks?
- What practical boundary conditions (sequencing depth, QC, parameter tuning) should be considered when applying LSI-based ArchR workflows to zebrafish snATAC-seq?

## Key findings
The protocol shows that applying an ArchR pipeline with TF‑IDF + SVD (LSI) dimensionality reduction, followed by peak calling and integration of a curated zebrafish motif PWM database, allows identification of cluster-specific accessible regions, enrichment of TF motifs in those regions, and TF footprinting signals consistent with dynamic TF binding during early embryogenesis. The paper is presented as an applied, reproducible pipeline rather than a benchmarking or algorithmic comparison; no quantitative performance benchmarks (e.g., accuracy, cell counts, peak counts) are reported in the summary.

## Methods used
ArchR pipeline; Latent Semantic Indexing (TF‑IDF normalization + SVD) for dimensionality reduction and clustering; sample integration across multiple snATAC-seq libraries; quality-control filtering; peak calling in ArchR; construction/curation of zebrafish TF motif PWMs; motif enrichment testing across peaks; TF footprinting analysis.

## Method and dataset
Methods applied to single-nucleus ATAC-seq (snATAC/scATAC) data from zebrafish early embryos, integrating multiple samples/stages. Exact dataset sizes (cell counts, number of samples) are not specified in the summary. Experimental design assumes multiple libraries to be integrated, sufficient sequencing depth to call peaks and compute LSI embeddings, and availability (or capability to curate) motif PWMs for zebrafish TFs.

## Limitations
Protocol paper (no novel biological discovery); does not present primary algorithmic derivation or comparative benchmarks for TF‑IDF+SVD/iterative LSI; lacks detailed mathematical treatment of depth-correction or explicit implementation details of iterative LSI; effectiveness depends on completeness and quality of curated zebrafish motif PWMs and on sequencing depth and sample quality; computational requirements and parameter tuning can affect results.

## Evidence pattern
Entity definition (worked protocol) and implementation-level demonstration; analysis used: ArchR TF‑IDF+SVD LSI, peak calling, motif enrichment, footprinting; boundary conditions flagged (sequencing depth, QC, parameter tuning); limited formal validation or comparative benchmarking provided in the protocol summary.

## Extends or contradicts
Extends and documents ArchR-based LSI workflows and applied motif-analysis steps for a non-model-species (zebrafish) use case; does not provide the primary methodological justification or comparative evaluation versus Signac/Stuart et al. 2021 or other iterative LSI approaches.

## Boundary conditions
Works when: Works when per-cell / per-nucleus sequencing depth is sufficient to call peaks and compute TF‑IDF normalization; when users can curate or obtain species-appropriate TF PWM matrices for zebrafish; when sample quality meets standard ArchR QC filters; when users can tune peak calling and clustering parameters appropriate to dataset.
Fails when: Fails or underperforms when sequencing depth per cell/nucleus is too low to support robust peak calling or LSI embeddings; when zebrafish motif PWMs are missing, incomplete, or low quality; when severe batch effects exist and no appropriate batch-correction/integration is performed; when users require algorithmic/benchmark-level guidance on depth-correction or iterative LSI not provided by the protocol.
